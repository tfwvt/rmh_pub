Robert M.
Herzog

Robert M. Herzog is a novelist, screenwriter, playwright, and filmmaker whose work explores ambition, identity, moral choices, and the contradictions of human behavior. Moving between drama, satire, and speculative fiction, his stories examine people navigating changing worlds, confronting unexpected challenges, and sometimes discovering that their greatest obstacles are themselves.

A published author and award-winning filmmaker, Herzog brings to his creative work decades of experience as an entrepreneur and executive in technology, energy, and healthcare, as well as a lifelong engagement with politics and American culture.
