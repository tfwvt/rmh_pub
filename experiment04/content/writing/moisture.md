---
title: Moisture
kind: poetry
venue: South Jersey Underground, Issue 11
---

The twinkling glaze over morning fields\
The wintry haze shrouding bedroom windows,\
a reminder of our own night’s endless breath.\
The aromatic wave of honeydew and wine.\
The leavener of heated rooms and day-old bread.\
The friend to mist and intercourse\
Conferring space for our own mysteries.\
It warms our hands, cools our brow,\
And raises our smallest prayers to heaven.
