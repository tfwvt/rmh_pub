# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed 2026-11-03):

- **Film backers and industry**: funders, producers, festival programmers and press looking at *No Kidding Around*. Their next step is to support the film through The Gotham's fiscal sponsorship, request the screenplay, or open the pitch deck.
- **Readers**: people buying Robert's books (*Views from the Side Mirror*, *A World Between*) and reading his essays, op-eds, fiction and poetry on the site, Substack and Medium. Their next step is to buy a book, read a piece, or subscribe to the newsletter.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Robert M. Herzog (novelist, screenwriter, essayist, playwright and filmmaker), replacing thezog.com. It presents him as a film writer and producer and, with equal weight, as a published author, and it hosts every piece of writing that was on thezog.com on its own reading page.

## Positioning

One person who writes and produces film (*No Kidding Around*, *Flights*) and publishes novels and essays. He describes himself as living "at this intersection of art and entrepreneurship". The film work and the books come from the same writer, and the site should show both.

## Operating Context

- Static site built by `build.js` from `content.js` and `content/writing/*.md`, deployed on DigitalOcean App Platform. Writing is edited in Pages CMS.
- Each `experimentNN/` folder is a design iteration of the same site and content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Redesigns keep the content and images exactly as they are (wording, facts, images). Sections and items may be reordered and regrouped.
- Writing filters (kind and search), "Show more", reading pages, the 404 redirects from old thezog.com addresses and the Pages CMS workflow have to keep working.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Robert M. Herzog. Contact: zog@thezog.com. Newsletter: Robert's Newsletter on Substack.
- Existing logos and badges are used as given: The Gotham Film & Media Institute (fiscal sponsor) and Austin Short Film Festival.

## Evidence on Hand

- Laurels: *No Kidding Around*: Finalist, Best Drama Screenplay, Austin Short Film Festival, Fall 2026; Semi-Finalist, Chicago Script Awards, 2026. *Flights*: Best Film, Short Subject, Cinevue International Film Competition; screened at 12 festivals.
- Quotes and reviews in `content.js` (Joyce Schwarz, Pamela Chomba, Rich Rubin and reader reviews of *A World Between*).
- *A World Between*: Amazon best seller and Barnes & Noble e-Book of the Day.
- Images in `assets/img/`, and the pitch deck at `assets/files/no-kidding-around-pitch-deck.pdf`.
- Don't invent any other awards, press, sales figures or quotes.

## Product Principles

1. Film and books each get a full stage; neither is a footnote to the other.
2. Proof first: laurels, sponsor, quotes and reviews sit right next to the work they support.
3. Every piece of writing is easy to find and comfortable to read.
4. Content stays exactly as written; only the presentation changes.
